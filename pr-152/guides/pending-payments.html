Pending payments ​

settlement_pending means the facilitator relayed the payer-signed transaction to Canton but does not know yet whether it committed: the participant took it, and then the answer was lost or slow. It is not a failure. The transaction may still commit, and if it does the payer has paid. Treating it as a failure is how a payer ends up paying twice.

What a retry tells you ​

The facilitator remembers every transaction it relayed. Presenting the same payment again, at verify or at settle, does not submit it again; it answers with what the facilitator knows:

The first relay…A retry of the same payment answers
is still unresolvedsettlement_pending
committedsettlement_pending
was rejected by the ledgerinvalid_exact_canton_execute_failed

A retry can tell you a payment failed, so the payer can sign a new one. It cannot tell you it succeeded: the spec forbids a facilitator from answering a settle from a remembered commit, so a committed payment keeps answering settlement_pending. Once the transfer's deadline has passed, a retry is refused as invalid_exact_canton_expired before any of this.

The answer names the transaction, in transaction on settle and in extra.transaction on verify. It is the payment's preparedTxHash, since there is no update id until it commits.

Merchants ​

The Express middleware answers a pending settlement with a 402 carrying only PAYMENT-RESPONSE: no new challenge, so a client is not invited to pay again. The request is not served, and onReject is not called, since nothing is owed an undo.

The funds may still land at your party. If they do, serve or refund the order out of band, matching it by the transaction hash or your memo.

Payers ​

  • Do not sign a new payment for a request answered settlement_pending. The first one may still move your funds.
  • Do not retry the request through createX402Fetch. The wrapper sends one payment and returns what comes back; called again, it gets a fresh 402 and signs a second transfer.
  • Check it with isSettlementPending from @chainsafe/x402-core, on the settle answer decoded from PAYMENT-RESPONSE (decodePaymentResponseHeader).
  • Re-sending the same PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header is safe, and tells you if the payment failed. If it keeps answering settlement_pending, take it up with the merchant, naming the transaction.

Calling the facilitator directly ​

FacilitatorClient.settle returns a pending answer as a value, not an error, since the facilitator sends it with HTTP 200. Settling the same payload again is safe: it never submits twice, and it answers invalid_exact_canton_execute_failed if the first relay was rejected. A payment that keeps answering settlement_pending has to be confirmed on the ledger, by its transaction.
